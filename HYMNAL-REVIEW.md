# Hymnal review

86 of 92 hymns, 82 English and 26 German texts, 86 charted.

Everything here is public domain, but that is a copyright fact, not an
editorial one. Stanza wording and stanza count differ between hymnals, German
orthography differs by era, and the chords are one playable reading of a tune
rather than a transcription of any particular setting. Mark up anything that
is wrong and it gets fixed at the source files in `data-prep/hymnal/`, which
is what `data/hymnal.json` is built from.

Chords are shown above the syllable they strike, which is where a wrong one
shows up. Sources are the pages actually consulted.

Tune **NICAEA** · 11.12.12.10 · key **D**

*Reginald Heber, 1826*

```
1.
D     Bm    A7 D   G          D
Holy, holy, ho ly! Lord God Almighty!
A     D  A   Bm                     E7      A
Early in the morning our song shall rise to Thee;
D     Bm    A7 D   G            D
Holy, holy, ho ly, merciful and mighty!
Bm     D7    G        D       A7   D
God in three Persons, blessèd Trinity!

2.
Holy, holy, holy! All the saints adore Thee,
Casting down their golden crowns around the glassy sea;
Cherubim and seraphim falling down before Thee,
Who was, and is, and evermore shall be.

3.
Holy, holy, holy! though the darkness hide Thee,
Though the eye of sinful man Thy glory may not see;
Only Thou art holy; there is none beside Thee,
Perfect in power, in love, and purity.

4.
Holy, holy, holy! Lord God Almighty!
All Thy works shall praise Thy name, in earth, and sky, and sea;
Holy, holy, holy; merciful and mighty!
God in three Persons, blessèd Trinity!

```

Sources: <http://hymntime.com/tch/htm/h/o/l/y/h/holyholy.htm> · <https://hymnary.org/text/holy_holy_holy_lord_god_almighty_early> · <https://hymnary.org/tune/nicaea_dykes> · <https://hymnchords.net/files/HOLY,%20HOLY,%20HOLY.pdf> · <https://library.timelesstruths.org/music/Holy_Holy_Holy/>

> **Sourcing note:** Text: Cyber Hymnal (Heber 1826), which prints each stanza on five typographic lines; reflowed here to the four metrical lines of 11.12.12.10, no words changed. Hymnary's representative text (Hymns and Devotions for Daily Worship #184a) agrees word for word apart from lowercased pronouns and "pow'r" for "power", and capitalises "Holy, Holy, Holy" inconsistently in stanza 3 -- which is why the Cyber Hymnal form was preferred. "blessèd" keeps the source's grave accent (sung as two syllables). Key: hymnary's incipit database lists NICAEA in E major, but Trinity Hymnal p.100 and the Hymn Fake Book both print it in D, so D was taken as the common hymnal key -- already a guitar key, no transposition needed. Chords are a simplification of the Trinity-keyed chart at hymnchords.net. SCANSION: only stanza 1's second line is the 12 syllables the meter names; stanzas 2-4 have 13 there ("Casting down their golden crowns around the glassy sea"), which the tune absorbs on a repeated note. Hymnary's instance list shows several hymnals giving up and calling the meter Irregular for this reason; 11.12.12.10 is what the text authority page states and is used here. VERIFIED 2026-08-02: cross-checked against Timeless Truths (URL above), which agrees word for word -- including stanza 2's "Who was, and is, and evermore shall be" -- apart from printing "pow'r", "blessed" and "Thy Name".
